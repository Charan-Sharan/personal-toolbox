Interpretation

Inflation-adjusted value is the projected corpus divided by cumulative inflation over the investment period. It expresses purchasing power in today’s currency. The calculator does not model tax, fund expenses, volatility, missed contributions, or changing returns. Consult the relevant scheme documents and consider your risk tolerance before investing.

How to make this estimate useful

1. Weigh only gold content, not the gross jewellery weight. 2. Use the lender’s purity test and net weight. 3. Enter the lender’s reference price per gram and applicable LTV or margin. 4. Choose the repayment option stated in the lender quote. 5. Enter fee amounts and rate from the current sanction terms.

How this calculator works

A monthly reducing-balance loan: interest = opening balance × annual rate ÷ 12; your EMI pays interest first, then principal. Extra repayments reduce the remaining principal, and each payment is capped at what is owed. The final EMI may be smaller.

In “finish earlier” mode, prepayments leave the EMI unchanged. In “lower EMI” mode, it is recalculated from the next month across the months left to the original maturity. Rate changes follow your chosen rate policy. A recurring payment can increase every 12 months from its start.

All fees and fee taxes are separate cash outflows. Totals use unrounded internal values; displayed and exported figures are rounded to two decimals, so small summation differences are possible. No daily interest, missed payments, moratorium, stage disbursements, flat-rate loans, insurance, tax benefits, or lender-specific rounding are modeled.

Planning estimate using your inputs. Check your lender’s amortization schedule, interest convention and applicable fees before acting. Loan categories are labels; they do not assume current rates or rules.
